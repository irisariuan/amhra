# Wake words for voice commands, one phrase per line. Read when the sidecar
# starts (with --voice-commands); restart it after editing.
#
# Write plain words. They are spelled into the keyword model's word pieces at
# load, and a word it cannot spell is refused with its line number.
#
# Optional settings follow the phrase:
#   :<boost>      pulls the decoder towards this phrase (default 1.5)
#   #<threshold>  confidence needed to fire, 0..1 (default 0.25); lower fires
#                 more readily, and falsely more often
#
# The model hears sounds, not spellings, so each line is a way of *saying* the
# name. Tuned on synthesised speech (rust/README.md has the numbers):
